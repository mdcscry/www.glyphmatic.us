Random line arrangements typically achieve far fewer triangles than the optimum. The known optima for many k values were found by hand (Wajnberg, Suzuki, Wood, Bartholdi) or by SAT solvers (Savchuk, 2025). Try k=31 to see how a random arrangement compares to the record of 299.
