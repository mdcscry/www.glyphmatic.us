1/4 + 1/4² + 1/4³ + ··· = 1/3

A 36-panel visual proof — the dark region in each square covers exactly 1/3 of its area

Each panel divides the square into 4 quadrants. One corner is shaded dark (= 1/4 of the area). The opposite corner gets divided again, and the same recursive shading repeats — yielding 1/4 + 1/16 + 1/64 + ··· The recursion's "anchor corner" sets the orientation; rotations and reflections give 4 visually distinct variants. Light cells make up exactly 2/3 of every panel — twice the dark region.
