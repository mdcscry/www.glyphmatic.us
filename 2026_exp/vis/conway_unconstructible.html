The 306-cell unconstructible still life

Törmä & Salo (2022), reduced by Cunningham — a Conway's Life pattern whose only predecessor is itself

Still life — the actual 306-cell pattern
Agar — the infinite parent it was carved from

Each tile renders the pattern as a binary grid: live cells in the dark color, dead cells in the light color. The still-life tiles show the full 306-cell construction at varying zooms and crops. The agar tiles show finite windows into the infinite spatially-periodic pattern that Törmä & Salo's SAT solver identified as having a unique chain of preimages — the still life is essentially a clipped, stabilized rectangle of this wallpaper.
