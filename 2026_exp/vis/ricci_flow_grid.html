Ricci flow — 50 evolutions

Surfaces of revolution evolving under ∂g/∂t = −2·Ric(g). Each row evolves a different starting shape; each column is a snapshot in time.

The radius profile r(z, t) evolves by smoothing out curvature variations — bumps shrink, necks thin (sometimes pinching), bulbs round. Without surgery, near-pinch shapes collapse to nothing; with surgery, they split into spheres. Each row shows ten time steps of one initial profile.
