Koebe–Andreev–Thurston Circle Packing

Every finite planar triangulation is the tangent graph of some circle packing

The Theorem: For any finite planar graph that triangulates a disk (with a designated outer face), there exists a circle packing where circles correspond to vertices and two circles are tangent if and only if their vertices are adjacent. The packing is unique up to Möbius transformations.
